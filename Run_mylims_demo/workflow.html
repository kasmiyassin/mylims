DNA Extraction Protocol

🧬 Biology Active

Standard protocol for DNA extraction from tissue samples using phenol-chloroform method. This protocol is optimized for fish tissue samples.

DNA Extraction Phenol-Chloroform Fish Tissue

Materials & Reagents

Phenol-Chloroform-Isoamyl Alcohol (25:24:1) Sigma-Aldrich, Cat# P2069
50 mL
Proteinase K Thermo Fisher, Cat# EO0491
100 mg
RNase A Qiagen, Cat# 19101
10 mg
Ethanol (100%) Merck, Cat# 100983
500 mL

Equipment

Thermal Shaker Eppendorf, Model 5430
Microcentrifuge Eppendorf, Model 5424R
NanoDrop Spectrophotometer Thermo Fisher, ND-1000

Procedure

1️⃣ Tissue Homogenization

Weigh approximately 25 mg of tissue and place in a 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tube. Add 180 µL of ATL buffer and 20 µL of Proteinase K. Vortex briefly and incubate at 56°C overnight with shaking at 900 rpm.

Duration: 16-18 hours
Temperature: 56°C
Safety Notes: Wear gloves and lab coat. Work in fume hood when handling Proteinase K.

2️⃣ Phenol-Chloroform Extraction

Add 200 µL of AL buffer to the sample and mix thoroughly by vortexing. Then add 200 µL of ethanol (96-100%) and mix again. Carefully apply the mixture to the DNeasy Mini spin column placed in a 2 mL collection tube.

Duration: 10 minutes
Safety Notes: Phenol-chloroform is toxic. Use only in fume hood with proper PPE.

3️⃣ Washing and Elution

Centrifuge at 8000 x g for 1 min. Discard flow-through and collection tube. Place the DNeasy Mini spin column in a new 2 mL collection tube, add 500 µL of AW1 buffer, and centrifuge for 1 min at 8000 x g. Discard flow-through. Place the DNeasy Mini spin column in a new 2 mL collection tube, add 500 µL of AW2 buffer, and centrifuge for 3 min at 14,000 x g to dry the membrane. Transfer the DNeasy Mini spin column to a clean 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tube and elute DNA with 100 µL of AE buffer. Incubate for 5 min at room temperature, then centrifuge for 1 min at 8000 x g.

Duration: 15 minutes

Expected Results

DNA yield should be between 5-20 µg from 25 mg of tissue. A260/A280 ratio should be between 1.8-2.0, indicating pure DNA. DNA should be visible as a high molecular weight band on a 0.8% agarose gel.

Parameter Expected Value Acceptable Range
DNA Concentration 50-200 ng/µL 20-500 ng/µL
A260/A280 Ratio 1.8-2.0 1.7-2.1
A260/A230 Ratio 2.0-2.2 1.8-2.4
